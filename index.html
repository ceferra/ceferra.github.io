About

I work on machine learning and artificial intelligence at the Valencian Research Institute for Artificial Intelligence (VRAIN), Universitat Politècnica de València. My research started on how to evaluate classifiers properly: ROC analysis, AUC, calibration, cost curves and threshold choice. It has since grown into a broader question: how to evaluate and predict the behaviour of AI systems, from classifiers to large language models and autonomous agents.

I also apply machine learning to real-world problems: urban mobility and air quality, environmental monitoring with computer vision, and education. I maintain several open, continuously updated datasets.

Research interests

ML evaluation

ROC analysis, AUC, performance metrics, calibration, cost curves and threshold choice.

Predictable & reliable AI

Measuring and anticipating the capabilities and failures of LLMs and AI agents.

Quantification

Estimating class prevalence under dataset shift for classification and regression.

Explainability & machine teaching

Interpretable models, XAI for time series, teaching size and inductive programming.

Data science processes

CRISP-DM and data science trajectories, automated data wrangling, size-constrained clustering.

Applied AI

Urban mobility and pollution, microfibre detection in wastewater, photo restoration, learning analytics.

Software & open data

  • València urban-mobility archive Continuously collected open data from València: Valenbisi bike-sharing, traffic intensity, loop detectors and parking occupancy (2015–today).
  • JCDecaux GBFS archive Daily archive of bike-share station status for all 19 cities operated by JCDecaux/Cyclocity.
  • Agent-trace dataset Agent-execution traces (Inspect AI) of LLM agents across coding and tool-use benchmarks.
  • ADeLe annotation distillation Multi-model demand-level annotation and a distilled 7B annotator for AI evaluation.
  • BSPA & EMQSPA Competitive quantification at low computational cost.
  • Microfibre segmentation quality Label-free estimation of instance-segmentation quality for microfibre detection.
  • Cost curves R tools for cost curves, ROC analysis and threshold-choice methods.
