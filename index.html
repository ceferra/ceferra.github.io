About

I am a Full Professor in the Department of Computer Systems and Computation at the Universitat Politècnica de València, and a member of the DMIP group at VRAIN, the Valencian Research Institute for Artificial Intelligence, where I have done research since 1999. I am also a member of ValgrAI, the Valencian Graduate School and Research Network of Artificial Intelligence. Since 2009 I have been Assistant Dean for University–Enterprise Relations at the School of Computer Science (ETSINF). I have also directed the Chair in Artificial Intelligence Applied to Public Administration (Cátedra de Inteligencia Artificial aplicada a la Administración Pública) since 2021, and the university extension diploma in Artificial Intelligence of the Samsung Innovation Campus since 2022.

My research began with how to evaluate classifiers properly: ROC analysis, AUC, calibration, cost curves and threshold choice. It has since grown into a broader question: how to evaluate and predict the behaviour of AI systems, from classifiers to large language models and autonomous agents. I also apply machine learning to real-world problems, including urban mobility, air quality, environmental monitoring and education, and I maintain several open, continuously updated datasets.

I received an MSc in Computer Science (1998) and a PhD (2003) from the Universitat Politècnica de València. My thesis was on applying declarative languages to machine learning.

Research interests

ML evaluation

ROC analysis, AUC, performance metrics, calibration, cost curves and threshold choice.

Predictable & reliable AI

Measuring and anticipating the capabilities and failures of LLMs and AI agents.

Quantification

Estimating class prevalence under dataset shift for classification and regression.

Explainability & machine teaching

Interpretable models, XAI for time series, teaching size and inductive programming.

Data science processes

CRISP-DM and data science trajectories, automated data wrangling, size-constrained clustering.

Applied AI

Urban mobility and pollution, microfibre detection in wastewater, photo restoration, learning analytics.
